INTERPRETATION
This is a transaction-selection result, not an entry-mode effect. Percent
sought is observed after the rupture. Firms without a sale attempt are
absent, and distributor or license relationships generally do not appear.
Reported values use different currencies and cannot be compared without
date-specific conversion. The file does not report accounting write-downs.
